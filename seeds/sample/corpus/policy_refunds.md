# Refund and Return Policy

§2.1 Standard Returns Window
The standard returns window is 30 days from delivery. All returned items must be in original condition with intact packaging.

§2.2 Restocking Fees
Returned electronics and opened software incur a standard 10% restocking fee.

§2.3 Expedition Equipment
Expedition-grade equipment carries a 60-day window. Items may be field-tested but must show no irreparable structural degradation.

§2.4 Exceptions and Warranty Claims
Damaged items reported within 48 hours of delivery receive an expedited replacement or full refund regardless of category.
