# Master Services Agreement - Meridian Global

Article 1(1) Scope of Work
Meridian Global shall supply specialized sensor assemblies and telemetry hardware.

§12.1 Renewal Terms
This agreement shall remain effective until terminated by mutual written consent or end of term.

§12.2 Expiration Date
The MSA term ends 31 March 2027. Any extension requires an executed, counter-signed written amendment.
