# Enterprise Pricing and Discount Policy

§5.1 Standard Approval Thresholds
Discounts above 15 percent require approval from the Regional Vice President of Sales.

§5.2 Tier 3 Dealer Discounts
Tier 3 dealers qualify for an automatic 12 percent baseline margin on catalog hardware.

§5.3 Custom Deal Structuring
Multi-year commitments exceeding €500k require co-authorization by Group Finance.
